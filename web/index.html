About Sakura

Sakura is MekaHime's first prototype, now open source. She runs on your own machine: a brain with a 26-emotion mood, favorability and per-user memory; a voice (a MekaHime build of GPT-SoVITS); ears (a MekaHime-modified SenseVoiceSmall); and a VRoid body on a three.js stage that acts out what she feels. The language model is your choice — OpenRouter in the cloud, Ollama locally, or any OpenAI-compatible server (llama.cpp, LM Studio, vLLM, ...).

Project page: mekahime.com/sakura · Source code: github.com/zeekk0/OpenSakura

Privacy

We collect nothing. OpenSakura runs on your machine. MekaHime Inc. operates no service for this project: no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports, no logs leave your machine. Her memory notes, user profiles and the cost ledger are plain JSON files in data/ on your disk.

Connections that do leave your computer, all visible in the code: (1) your LLM provider, if you choose OpenRouter — your messages, her persona and the memory notes relevant to the conversation go from your computer straight to OpenRouter/remote OpenAI-compatible provider under their terms and never touch MekaHime (with every role on Ollama, or on an OpenAI-compatible server running on your own machine, nothing leaves at all; (2) DuckDuckGo, when Sakura decides to look something up or fetch a picture for a friend or owner (the query is printed in the console), and pictures she finds are loaded from their original sites; (3) the three.js libraries from a public CDN; (4) one-time model downloads (SenseVoice weights, Ollama models) and OpenRouter's public price list for the cost counter.
